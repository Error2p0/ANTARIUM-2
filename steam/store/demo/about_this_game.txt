[i]Long ago, the Old Colony filled these depths. Then the fire below grew restless, and before it fell silent, the Old Colony sealed the deep behind it.[/i]

[i]Now, under the grass, a new queen begins to dig.[/i]

[b]This is the free demo of Antarium 2[/b], an active incremental game and a colony automation sim in one. Found your first colony in a hole in a meadow and grow it into a living machine of tunnels, silk belts and busy chambers, all the way down to the Ancient Seal at the bottom of the stone strata.

Every system works, there is no time limit, and [b]your colony carries over to the full game[/b].

[h2]What's in the demo[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Your first colony[/b], from the meadow down to the Ancient Seal: [b]Topsoil and Roots[/b], [b]Clay and Groundwater[/b] and the [b]Stone Strata[/b], with the hidden cavern of the [b]Elder Tree[/b].
[*][b]9 of the 10 castes[/b], [b]39 of the 46 research discoveries[/b] and [b]22 of the 26 buildings[/b].
[*]Day and night, rain, thunderstorms and starfall nights, raids, random events and offline progress.
[*]A tutorial for your first minutes and a help guide that grows with your colony (F1).
[/list]

[h2]Dig, and the colony follows[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_dig[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Click and hold to dig[/b] yourself: debris flies, the screen shakes and a combo builds while you keep breaking rock. Pick a brush size, and mind your strength.
[*][b]Paint orders, not ants.[/b] Mark rock for mining and paint storage zones. Workers take the jobs, haul every find home and climb walls and ceilings to get there.
[*]Every item is physical: ore piles up where it falls, sits on storage shelves and rides on belts.
[/list]

[h2]Build a living machine[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_factory[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Silk and iron belts, root lifts, splitters, filters, chests and unloaders[/b] move ore from the mining front to your machines, and the items visibly ride along.
[*][b]Smelters, crushers, fungus gardens, aphid ranches, archives and nurseries[/b] turn raw finds into copper, iron, food, honeydew and knowledge.
[*][b]Put the water to work:[/b] pumps move it, water wheels speed up nearby machines and irrigation grows bigger harvests.
[*][b]Flow, light and ore overlays[/b] show what is moving, what is jammed, where it is dark and where the ore lies.
[/list]

[h2]A world above and below[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_sky[/img]
[list]
[*]Your colony lives under [b]a real sky[/b]: day and night, rolling hills, trees with deep roots, lakes and rivers.
[*][b]Rain and thunderstorms[/b] pour water into careless tunnels. On rare [b]starfall nights[/b] shooting stars fill the sky and the hunters stay home.
[*][b]Forage the wild:[/b] seeds, berries, apples and toadstools on the surface, and mushrooms that glow in the caves below.
[*]Groundwater floods, sand caves in, and fire spreads through coal seams and roots. Bring [b]glow ants[/b] to light the dark.
[/list]

[h2]Take control[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_possess[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Possess any ant[/b] and play it yourself: run, tunnel, carry and fight, with a warm light in the dark.
[*][b]Paint pheromones:[/b] trails that speed ants up, scents that pull them to digging, gathering or defending, and areas to avoid.
[*][b]Set the caste mix[/b] and the queen lays it: workers, miners, haulers, builders, nurses, scouts, engineers, soldiers and glow ants.
[*][b]Evolve your queen[/b], set job priorities, and use [b]Frenzy, Seismic Sense, Royal Decree and Pheromone Burst[/b] when it counts.
[/list]

[h2]The Ancient Seal[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_seal[/img]
At the bottom of the stone strata your ants hit the [b]Ancient Seal[/b], where the Old Colony closed off the deep. This is where the demo ends, but you get a look through it first.

When you buy the full game, it offers to [b]continue your demo colony[/b] with its ants, tunnels and progress, and the seal opens. Keep playing the demo as long as you like: nothing is lost.
