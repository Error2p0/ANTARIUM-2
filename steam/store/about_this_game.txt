[i]Long ago, the Old Colony filled these depths. Its tunnels ran from the roots down to the fire, and at the bottom of everything it built the Engine, a machine of iron and singing crystal that let the whole colony think as one. Then the fire below grew restless, and the Engine fell silent.[/i]

[i]Now, under the grass, a new queen begins to dig.[/i]

You are the mind of that colony. It starts as a queen and a handful of workers in a hole in a meadow. It grows into a living machine: tunnels, silk belts and busy chambers reaching down through crystal caves and lava to the ruins of the colony that came before. Then your princesses take to the sky, and it all begins again, stronger.

[b]Antarium 2[/b] is an active incremental game and a colony automation sim in one. Dig by hand when you want to, automate it once you have mastered it, and watch hundreds of ants carry your colony forward.

[h2]Dig, and the colony follows[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_dig.gif[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Click and hold to dig[/b] yourself: debris flies, the screen shakes and a combo builds while you keep breaking rock. Pick a brush size, and mind your strength.
[*][b]Paint orders, not ants.[/b] Mark rock for mining and paint storage zones. Workers take the jobs, haul every find home and climb walls and ceilings to get there.
[*]Every item is physical: ore piles up where it falls, sits on storage shelves and rides on belts.
[/list]

[h2]Build a living machine[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_factory.gif[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Silk and iron belts, root lifts, splitters, filters, chests and unloaders[/b] move ore from the mining front to your machines, and the items visibly ride along.
[*][b]Smelters, crushers, fungus gardens, aphid ranches, archives, nurseries and princess chambers[/b] turn raw finds into copper, iron, food, honeydew, knowledge and royalty.
[*][b]Put the world to work:[/b] pumps move water, water wheels speed up nearby machines, irrigation grows bigger harvests, and a smelter built on magma needs no coal.
[*][b]Flow, light and ore overlays[/b] show what is moving, what is jammed, where it is dark and where the ore lies, so tuning your lines feels like a puzzle.
[/list]

[list]

[*][b]Forage the wild:[/b] seeds, berries, apples and toadstools on the surface, and mushrooms that glow in the caves below.

[h2]Take control[/h2]
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/antarium_possess.gif[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Possess any ant[/b] and play it yourself: run, tunnel, carry and fight, with a warm light in the dark.
[*][b]Paint pheromones:[/b] trails that speed ants up, scents that pull them to digging, gathering or defending, and areas to avoid.
[*][b]Ten castes:[/b] workers, miners, haulers, builders, nurses, scouts, engineers, soldiers, glow ants and fire ants. Set the mix and the queen lays it.
[*][b]Evolve your queen[/b], set job priorities, and watch individual ants gain skill, levels and traits.
[*][b]Abilities and events:[/b] Frenzy, Seismic Sense, Royal Decree, Pheromone Burst and Quickening; meteorite strikes, rich veins, golden beetles and trapped ants to rescue.
