Generated by *Antarium → Steam → Export Achievements and Icons* from `Content/Achievements`. Do not edit by hand: change the assets and export again.
Enter these on partner.steamgames.com → your app → **Edit Steamworks Settings → Stats & Achievements**, then **Publish** (Publish tab). See `docs/STEAM.md`.
